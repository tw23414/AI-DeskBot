# AI-DeskBot – Project Plan

## 1. Project Vision

AI-DeskBot is a personal AI desktop robot designed to become an intelligent, interactive companion.

The goal is to create an AI with:

- Its own consistent personality and identity.
- Long-term memory that develops over time.
- Voice conversations and natural communication.
- The ability to control and interact with a PC.
- A physical robotic body with movement, eyes, a camera and sensors.
- The ability to observe its surroundings and recognise objects.
- A security system to protect its computer and data.
- The ability to communicate with other AI robots.

AI-DeskBot should be able to carry out everyday tasks independently while asking for confirmation when an action requires it.

The project will be developed gradually, starting as a desktop AI assistant before expanding into a physical robot.

## 2. Main Development Goal

Build a working desktop AI assistant first, then gradually introduce the personality, memory, PC integration, physical hardware and advanced features.

## 3. Development Stages

1. Desktop AI assistant.
2. Personality and memory.
3. PC integration.
4. Physical robot.
5. Camera and recognition.
6. Companion app.
7. Battery and charging dock.
8. Advanced intelligence and expansion.

## 4. Project Principles

- Keep the AI's personality and identity consistent.
- Protect private information and memories.
- Keep important systems secure.
- Build features in manageable stages.
- Document progress and changes.
- Keep future expansion possible.
